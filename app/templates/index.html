Getting a reliable result

  • One leaf, filling the frame. Several leaves at once splits the model's attention.
  • Even daylight. The model's known weak point is low light, and it will warn you when confidence drops.
  • Plain background. Soil and other plant parts occluding the leaf cause most misclassifications.
  • Focus on the symptoms. Centre the spots, mould or discolouration.
